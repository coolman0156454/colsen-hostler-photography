Privacy Policy — Lightroom MHS POST

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Lightroom MHS POST is a private media publishing and workflow application used to process images from Adobe Lightroom and assist with publishing content to connected Meta/Facebook destinations.

Information We Collect

Depending on how the application is used, we may process Discord identifiers, Meta account and destination identifiers, OAuth credentials, Lightroom share URLs, image files and metadata, publishing job information, and operational logs.

How We Use Information

Information is used to process Lightroom media, validate and transform images, detect duplicates, manage publishing jobs, connect authorized Meta destinations, assist with publishing, verify results, troubleshoot errors, and maintain application security.

Meta Information

When a Meta account is connected, the application may receive information according to the permissions granted during Meta authorization. Meta information is used only to provide the requested application functionality. We do not sell Meta user information.

Adobe Lightroom

The application may process public Adobe Lightroom share URLs submitted by an authorized user. Users are responsible for ensuring that they have permission to access and publish submitted media.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained as necessary to operate publishing jobs, maintain application state, troubleshoot issues, and maintain security. To request deletion of applicable information, contact colsenhostler1@gmail.com.

Third-Party Services

The application may interact with Meta/Facebook, Adobe Lightroom, Discord, and hosting or storage providers used to operate the application.

Changes

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will remain available at this URL.
